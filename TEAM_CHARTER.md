## Team Goals

### Quality Standards
- Target: Minimum 8/10 grade for BlockC
- Quality benchmark: 80% quality level per task/solution
- Prioritize delivering value over perfection

### Learning Objectives
- Develop collaborative research skills
- Build individual research trajectories within shared topic
- Strengthen project management and leadership abilities

---

## Communication Norms

### Primary Channel
- **Microsoft Teams Channel** - all project communication

### Response Times
- **Working hours:** 10:00-17:00
- **Expected response time:** Within 1 hour during working hours
- **Outside working hours:** Post in Teams, will be addressed the following day
- **Unavailability:** Team members determine their own availability and communicate accordingly

### Urgent Matters
- Post in Teams channel
- If outside working hours, will be addressed next working day

---

## Meeting Expectations

### Scheduled Meetings
- **Mandatory:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday at 10:00
- **Additional meetings:** As needed, by team agreement
- **Typical duration:** 60 minutes maximum

### Meeting Preparation (Required)
Before each meeting, all team members must:
- Read the meeting agenda
- Review Trello board
- Check Teams chat for updates

### Attendance and Punctuality
- Attendance at scheduled meetings is expected
- Advance notice required for absences
- Meetings start on time at 10:00

### Meeting Protocol
- Facilitator: Rotates (to be established)
- Minutes: Rotate alphabetically by last name
- End-of-meeting check: Round-robin commitment statements

---

## Work Quality Standards

### Definition of "Good Enough"
- Minimum 8/10 quality for the block overall
- 80% quality level per individual task or solution
- Focus on delivering value, not perfection
- High-achieving team standard while managing scope

### Review Process
- All work must be peer-reviewed before completion
- See CONTRIBUTING.md in repository for detailed workflow

---

## Accountability

### Missed Deadlines
**Protocol when unable to meet a commitment:**
1. Notify team as soon as possible
2. Communicate clearly when delivery will be possible
3. Do not wait until deadline to inform team

### Work Distribution
- Tasks assigned through Trello
- Balance workload across team
- Speak up if overloaded

---

## Tools and Workflows

### Trello
**Update expectations:**
- Every team member updates their assigned tasks daily
- Update before daily meetings

**Task completion criteria:**
A task is marked as DONE only when ALL of the following are complete:
- All checklist items within the card are checked off
- Task is marked complete with checkmark
- Work has been peer-reviewed
- Feedback has been received and addressed

**Board maintenance:**
- Keep cards current
- Move cards through workflow stages promptly
- Tag appropriately with ILOs and deadlines

### Git Repository
- **Branch protection:** Main branch is protected (non-negotiable)
- **Workflow details:** See CONTRIBUTING.md in repository
- **File organization:** All work products stored in repo

## Decision Making

### Process
1. **Primary method:** Reach consensus through discussion
2. **Fallback:** Majority vote when consensus cannot be achieved
3. **Authority:** Decisions are made by the whole group

### Decision Types
- Strategic decisions: Always seek consensus
- Tactical decisions: Majority vote acceptable
- Technical decisions: Defer to expertise, but group validates
